1. Legal Case Outcome Predictor

academic research / Jan-May 2025 / lead data developer

“What does a higher accuracy score actually hide?”

An end-to-end NLP classification pipeline in Python (LegalBERT, scikit-learn), tested on 658 cases. It compares Logistic Regression with Random Forest.

accuracy on 658 test cases      (1 # = 4%)
logistic regression |#############    50.9%
random forest       |################ 62.5%
                     gain: 11.6 points
  • Logistic Regression: 50.9% accuracy, 0.49 macro F1. Random Forest: 62.5% accuracy.
  • The 11.6-point gain hid a collapse: minority-class recall fell from 0.4 to 0.05, caused by majority-class bias.
  • Next step: lexical and gap analysis to find high-correlation legal terms and ideological variance drivers, defining domain-embedding needs for future versions.
